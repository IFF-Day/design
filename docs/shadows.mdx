## Two shadows

The site separates layers mostly with grounds and dividers, so there are only two shadows. A drop shadow barely shows in dark mode, so the dark popup shadow adds a 1px `#3D3D3D` outline that draws the edge.

- **Popup** `--shadow-pop` Menus, selects, dialogs and toasts: only what floats above the page.
- **Card** `--shadow-card` A 1px inner outline with a touch of drop shadow: the toggles under a poster, the selected segment.
