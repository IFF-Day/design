## The mark

The mark is a shape with no letters: two near-black planes and a red square, called the fold. On dark grounds the planes turn Programme #F5F5F5 and the square stays Admit red.

## The lockup

The lockup sets the mark beside IFFDAY in Archivo ExtraBold, converted to outlines. The site header uses the small horizontal lockup at every width. Horizontal, vertical and header sizes are in the brand kit, `iffday-brand-v2`, under `svg/lockup/` and `png/lockup/`.

## Icons

The favicon puts the mark on a rounded tile that follows the system appearance. The bitmap favicons widen the gaps between the planes, so the mark survives at 16px. The iOS app icon is an Icon Composer file in `apps/ios`, and the system draws its glass, dark and tinted looks.

## Geometry

Three paths in a 533 by 563 box. Redraw from these, never from a screenshot.

## Rules

- Outside the iOS app icon, the mark is always flat: no glass, gradient or shadow.
- The three colors of the mark never change; on dark grounds only the planes switch to Programme.
- The identity draws on cinema and festivals, never on national or cultural motifs.
- Colors and type are on [Colors](https://design.iff.day/colors) and [Typography](https://design.iff.day/typography); the source of record is `docs/brand.md`.
