## Controls (19)

Interactive controls from @iffday/ui, built on Base UI, which handles focus, keyboard use and screen readers.

- [Button](https://design.iff.day/components/button): Four looks, two sizes, a pill shape and the round icon-button.
- [Input](https://design.iff.day/components/input): Single-line inputs and Textarea, at the five control sizes buttons use.
- [InputGroup](https://design.iff.day/components/input-group): An icon and an input in one field: every search box on the site.
- [Field](https://design.iff.day/components/field): A label, a control and a hint make a field; Fieldset and Form group fields into a form.
- [Select](https://design.iff.day/components/select): A dropdown whose options are SelectOption children, as a field or inline in a row of filters.
- [CheckLabel](https://design.iff.day/components/checkbox): A checkbox with its label, clickable across the whole row.
- [ToggleButton](https://design.iff.day/components/toggle-button): A button with a pressed and an unpressed state, for marks like Watchlist and Like.
- [Tabs](https://design.iff.day/components/tabs): In-page tabs, with a line under the selected one.
- [Disclosure](https://design.iff.day/components/disclosure): A heading that shows or hides its content; hidden content stays in the page.
- [Menu](https://design.iff.day/components/menu): A menu of actions opened from a button, with group labels, separators and links.
- [Dialog](https://design.iff.day/components/dialog): A modal dialog; on narrow screens the same dialog becomes a bottom sheet.
- [Popover](https://design.iff.day/components/popover): A non-modal popup anchored to the button that opens it.
- [NavigationDrawer](https://design.iff.day/components/navigation-drawer): The phone navigation sheet that rises from the bottom and closes itself at 768px.
- [Toast](https://design.iff.day/components/toast): One notification at the bottom of the screen, gone after 6.5 seconds.
- [Pill](https://design.iff.day/components/pill): Small labels for short facts: a language, a section, a status.
- [Stars](https://design.iff.day/components/stars): Ratings in half stars, and StarRating, a rating input that works from the keyboard.
- [States](https://design.iff.day/components/states): Placeholders for empty and failed content.
- [Skeleton](https://design.iff.day/components/skeleton): Grey blocks the size of content still loading, so a page opens before its data.
- [PageHeading](https://design.iff.day/components/page-heading): The title, description and action of personal tool pages such as My plans.

## Film and community (22)

Page blocks from apps/web: posters, film tiles, ratings and reviews, built from the controls.

- [Avatar](https://design.iff.day/components/avatar): An initial in a circle, its color derived from the person, the same on every page.
- [Poster](https://design.iff.day/components/poster): The poster for the reader’s language, the title on a tint without one, a blank card without a film.
- [FilmTile](https://design.iff.day/components/film-tile): A poster with a title and a second line, with an optional rank and badge.
- [Shelf](https://design.iff.day/components/shelf): A row of films that scrolls sideways and runs to the screen edge on phones.
- [PosterStack](https://design.iff.day/components/poster-stack): A list cover: up to five overlapping posters, with empty slots kept grey.
- [MovieCard](https://design.iff.day/components/movie-card): The programme card: poster, title, director and a Watchlist heart.
- [FilmHeader](https://design.iff.day/components/film-header): The still across the top of a film page, framed on its focus and faded into the page.
- [FilmPosterGallery](https://design.iff.day/components/poster-gallery): Every poster of a film, filtered by language.
- [SectionHeader](https://design.iff.day/components/section-header): A section title with an optional count and a See all link.
- [DisplayTitle](https://design.iff.day/components/display-title): The large title of browsing pages: Feed, Screenings, Search and the festival directory.
- [Stats](https://design.iff.day/components/stats): A row of numbers with labels, such as a film’s Watchlist count and score.
- [ActionPanel](https://design.iff.day/components/action-panel): The grey panel of actions beside a film page’s poster, one action per row.
- [CategoryTiles](https://design.iff.day/components/category-tiles): Tiles with an icon that lead to the parts of a page.
- [RatingHistogram](https://design.iff.day/components/rating-histogram): Ratings in ten half-star bins, with the mean and the count.
- [CombinedScore](https://design.iff.day/components/combined-score): A film’s overall score out of 10: IFFDAY’s share against the other platforms, each with its own rating.
- [ScoreSummary](https://design.iff.day/components/score-summary): An entry’s score, star mean, red and black stickers and distribution.
- [RatingInput](https://design.iff.day/components/rating-input): A rating input for forms: a star preview beside a dropdown.
- [ReviewCard](https://design.iff.day/components/review-card): The layout of one review: avatar, byline, stars, text and actions.
- [ShortReview](https://design.iff.day/components/short-review): An audience review on an entry, with a spoiler shield, replies, withdrawal and reports.
- [ExpandableText](https://design.iff.day/components/expandable-text): Long text clamped to a few lines, with More to open the rest.
- [Spoiler](https://design.iff.day/components/spoiler): Content with spoilers, hidden until the reader asks for it.
- [TimeAgo](https://design.iff.day/components/time-ago): Relative times like 3 days ago, with the UTC time to the minute on hover.
