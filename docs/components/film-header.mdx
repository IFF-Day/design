## Example

The banner at the top of a film page. The still is scaled and moved around the point in `header.focus`, so people stay in frame, and ends on a hard edge above the title and poster. A 3:2 still loses at most 16% of its height, which brings it to 16:9, the frame BIFF’s own site uses; a window wider than that fills its sides with a blurred copy.

<Example name="components/film-header/basic" stage="fill">

On a film page the banner runs the full width of the window under the header; in this frame it is fitted to the frame’s width.

</Example>

## Usage

- The banner takes landscape stills only, never a poster. A film without a still has no banner.
