## Example

`Menu` opens 6px below its trigger and is at least 210px wide. Focus moves into the menu; the arrow keys move through it, typing jumps to a matching item, and Escape or a click outside closes it and returns focus to the button. Base UI handles all of this.

## Usage

- Pass the trigger as `trigger`; it keeps its own look. An icon-only trigger needs an `aria-label`.
- For navigation use `MenuLinkItem` with the site’s `Link` in `render`; the menu closes itself after the click.
- For a one-of choice, such as a sort order, put `MenuRadioItem`s in a `MenuRadioGroup` with `value` and `onValueChange`; the chosen item is ticked and the menu closes on a choice.
- Any item (`MenuItem`, `MenuLinkItem`, `MenuRadioItem`) takes a `description`: a quieter second line under its label, such as "Translated by AI" under a language.
- `MenuLabel` titles a group and `MenuSeparator` divides groups. Put actions with serious consequences, such as Withdraw and Report, in the last group.
