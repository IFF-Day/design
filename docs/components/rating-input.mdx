## Example

The five stars on the left only preview the value; the dropdown on the right is the input, with No rating and half a star to five stars, 11 options. The stars cannot be clicked, which suits a form that submits the rating with other fields.

<Example name="components/rating-input/basic" height={160} />

## Usage

To rate by clicking stars on a film page, use [StarRating](https://design.iff.day/components/stars). `onChange` receives half stars from 1 to 10, or `null` for No rating.
