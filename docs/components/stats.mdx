## Example

Values are subheading size with tabular figures, so neighbouring numbers line up; labels are micro grey text underneath. `icon` goes before the value in the star’s brand red. Under a festival entry’s title they show the Watchlist count, the average rating, this edition’s score and the film’s overall score; a person’s page shows their films and festival entries.
