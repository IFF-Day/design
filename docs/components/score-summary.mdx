## Example

An entry’s ratings in full: the score out of 10 with the number of people and whether the sample is large enough, the star mean, the red and black sticker counts for Recommended and Not recommended, then the star distribution and when it was calculated.

<Example name="components/score-summary/basic" />

## Usage

- `sampleStatus` is `ready`, `insufficient` or `none`, shown as individual responses, Not enough ratings and No ratings yet.
- The calculation time is always shown in Seoul time, a leftover from the BIFF-only days.
