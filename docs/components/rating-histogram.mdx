## Example

Ten bars run from half a star to five stars. The tallest fills 46px and the rest follow the counts; any bin with a rating keeps at least 6% height, so it never reads as zero. Hovering a bar shows 4 stars, 62 people. The mean and the count sit at the top.

## Usage

`distribution` has exactly ten numbers, the first for half a star. For an entry’s complete rating section use [ScoreSummary](https://design.iff.day/components/score-summary).
