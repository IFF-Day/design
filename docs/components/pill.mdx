## Example

`Pill` is a caption-size label; `tone` is `neutral` (grey, the default), `accent`, `warning` or `new`. `tone="accent"` is red text on a pale red ground, for things the reader should notice, such as a screening with a guest Q&A or an unread notification. `tone="warning"` is Signal yellow and only warns, for example of a cancelled screening. Put a row of pills in `.pill-row`, which handles wrapping and spacing.

## Usage

- Pills hold a word or two: a language, a section, a status. A sentence does not belong in one.
- `tone="new"` is micro and bold, an unread marker; no page uses it yet.
