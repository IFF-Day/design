## Example

The overall score out of 10 sits at the top, with how much of it comes from IFFDAY. Below it, one row per source: IFFDAY with its number of raters, then each platform in its own scale (stars out of 5, a score out of 10 or 100, a Tomatometer percentage) with its count and its share of the score. A platform with too few ratings is listed in grey as not counted.

## Without IFFDAY ratings

A festival premiere with no IFFDAY ratings yet still shows the score the other platforms give, and says so. Under five IFFDAY raters the line says how many there are.

## Usage

Pass `combined` from `GET /films/:id/rating`. Film pages and festival entry pages put it in the sidebar above the [RatingHistogram](https://design.iff.day/components/rating-histogram); entry pages title that one This edition, since an entry’s own score only counts IFFDAY ratings given there.
