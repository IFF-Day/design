## Example

An audience review on an entry page. It adds four things to [ReviewCard](https://design.iff.day/components/review-card): a review marked as a spoiler stays covered and its text is requested from the API only when opened; Reply opens the replies and a reply box; the menu lets the author withdraw the review and anyone report it; long reviews show eight lines and then More.

<Example name="components/short-review/basic" stage="fill">

The second review is marked as a spoiler. Replies and reports show a toast and are not saved.

</Example>

## Usage

`reload` is called after a reply or withdrawal succeeds, so the page reads its reviews again. `stars` is the author’s rating of the film, looked up by the page and passed in.
