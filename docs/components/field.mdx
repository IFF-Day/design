## Field

`Field` stacks a label, a control and a hint: the label is 13px and medium weight, the hint is secondary text. Base UI ties the label to the control, so a screen reader reads the label as the control’s name.

## Form

`Form` checks constraints such as `required` and `pattern` before it submits, and the browser points at the field that fails. Group related fields in a `Fieldset` named by a `Legend`. After a save, tell the reader with a toast: Save in the example below shows Saved.

## On a grey panel

A grey field vanishes on a grey panel. The panel sets four tokens, and every field inside it turns white with a hairline: `--field` and `--field-hover` to `var(--surface)`, `--field-line` to `var(--line)` and `--field-line-hover` to `var(--line-strong)`. The tool panels, the article editor, the review composer and the refund form do this.
